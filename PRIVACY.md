# Privacy Statement &mdash; Credit Mastery Challenge

**App:** WGRALGO Credit Mastery Challenge
**Publisher:** WGRALGO &mdash; The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm&trade; Inc.
**Package:** `org.wgralgo.creditmasterychallenge`
**Version:** 2.0.0

## What this app collects

**Nothing.**

- No account required
- No ads
- No analytics
- No trackers
- No data selling
- No cloud upload
- No personal financial data is collected
- No answers or scores are sent to WGRALGO or anywhere else
- The app is an **offline educational credit-awareness game**

## Android permissions

This APK does **not** declare the `INTERNET` permission (it is stripped from the final manifest). It also does not
declare `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` or any tracking/analytics permissions.

## Local data

Scores and progress within a single quiz round are kept only in app memory.
They are not written to long-term storage and never leave the device.

## Educational disclaimer

Credit Mastery Challenge is for educational awareness only. It does not
provide legal, financial, credit repair, lending, banking, or debt-management
advice. Credit scoring models vary, and individual situations are different.
For personal financial decisions, consult a qualified professional or the
relevant financial institution.
