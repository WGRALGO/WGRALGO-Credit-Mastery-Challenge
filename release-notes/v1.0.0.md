# Credit Mastery Challenge v1.0.0

First GitHub-ready release of the WGRALGO Credit Mastery Challenge Android
app.

## Highlights

- Offline 50-question credit-knowledge quiz with 10 randomized questions per
  round
- Difficulty + category badges, instant feedback, explanations, and credit
  tips
- Final mastery rating: Credit Master, Credit Builder, Money Student, or
  Needs More Practice
- Black and gold WGRALGO design language
- Fully offline &mdash; no `INTERNET` permission, no ads, no analytics, no
  trackers

## Install

1. Download `CreditMasteryChallenge-v1.0.0.apk`.
2. Optionally verify the SHA-256 against
   `CreditMasteryChallenge-v1.0.0.apk.sha256`.
3. Allow installs from unknown sources on your Android device and open the
   APK.
