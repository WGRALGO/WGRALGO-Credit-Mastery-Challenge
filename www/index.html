About

Credit Mastery Challenge is a free credit quiz from WGRALGO / The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm™ Inc. Test what you know about credit scores, credit cards, debt, and protecting your identity, and learn to spot common credit myths.

How it works

  • Pick a level: All Levels, Beginner, Intermediate, or Expert.
  • Answer 10 random questions from a bank of 60.
  • Every answer explains why, and wrong answers that are common myths get a myth buster.

For learning only. This is not financial or legal advice.
