How It Works

Credit Mastery Challenge is an offline educational quiz with a bank of 50 credit-knowledge questions.

  • Each round randomly selects 10 questions from the 50-question bank.
  • Pick the best answer from four options.
  • You get instant feedback, the correct answer, an explanation, and a short credit tip.
  • Your score is tallied at the end with a mastery rating.
  • No accounts. No internet. No tracking.

This app does not provide legal, financial, credit repair, lending, banking, or debt-management advice.

Privacy & Disclaimer
